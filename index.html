Hub 個人工具

這是擁有者自己使用的筆記與行程整理工具。它透過 Google 授權讀寫擁有者本人的 Google 日曆、雲端硬碟、文件與試算表，用來整理課表、作業與筆記。不對外提供服務，也不開放其他人註冊。

Hub Personal Tool

A personal note and schedule tool used only by its owner. With Google authorization it reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets to organise classes, assignments and notes. It is not offered to the public.
